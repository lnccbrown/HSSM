# Suggest a public HSSM story or teaching artifact

Use the public [content-suggestion issue form](https://github.com/lnccbrown/HSSM/issues/new?template=content-suggestion.yml)
when a method, release, tutorial, reproducibility artifact or community contribution
would benefit from a clearer explanation or visual.

Good suggestions name:

- one audience question or non-obvious premise;
- a canonical public source that supports it;
- what evidence is still missing;
- the contrast or mechanism a useful visual should reveal; and
- relevant scientific, clinical, licensing, privacy or attribution risks.

GitHub issues are public. Do not submit credentials, direct identifiers,
patient-level data, confidential partner information, raw unpublished results, or a
private conversation. A suggestion is intake only: it does not authorize drafting,
outward visibility, publication, or use of a person's name or likeness.
