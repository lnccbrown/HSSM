# Use and licensing

The authoritative terms are the repository's [LICENSE](license.md). In summary,
the text grants permission to use, copy, modify and distribute HSSM and its
documentation without fee except for incorporation into a commercial product or
service, subject to its notice conditions. It also restricts use of the Brown
University name in advertising or publicity without specific written permission.

This summary is a routing aid, not legal advice, a new licence, or permission for a
particular use. HSSM is publicly available scientific software and is not
represented as OSI open source.

## Route a question

1. Read the exact [licence text](license.md).
2. For a non-confidential clarification about project behavior or how to reach the
   maintainers, open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/lnccbrown/HSSM/discussions).
3. Do not post confidential product, partner, patient, or unpublished study details.
   State only that you need the authorized commercial-use route; the maintainers
   will route the question after the responsible university contact is confirmed.

The project does not promise a commercial licence, sponsored engagement, validation
or service through this page. Public wording and the durable commercial contact remain
subject to project and authorized university review.
