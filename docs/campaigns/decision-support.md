# HSSM in decision-support work

HSSM can help analyze how choices and response times relate to latent decision
processes, with hierarchical uncertainty and posterior checks. Whether that
analysis is useful depends on a named context of use: population, task, data
quality, estimand, decision threshold, alternatives and failure modes.

## Demonstrated today

- hierarchical sequential-sampling models and parameter-wise regressions;
- simulation through `hssm.simulate_data` and reproducible model specifications;
- posterior predictive checks, model comparison and uncertainty visualization;
- public tutorials and versioned software artifacts.

## Not established by software capability alone

- clinical validity, transportability or measurement invariance;
- regulatory acceptance or suitability for a submission;
- a causal mechanism from a fitted latent parameter;
- an organizational go/no-go threshold or utility function;
- permission to incorporate HSSM into a commercial product or service.

A public reference demonstrator must predeclare its question, threshold, simulated
operating characteristics, diagnostics and limitations. Until that reviewed
artifact exists, application examples are hypotheses and educational simulations,
not case studies or validation claims.

Before proposing an organizational evaluation, read
[use and licensing](../use-and-licensing.md). Bring non-confidential technical
questions to [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/lnccbrown/HSSM/discussions).
