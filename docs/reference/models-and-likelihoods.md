## Identifiability of inverse-temperature softmax models

For `softmax_inv_temperature_<n>` models, the choice probabilities are proportional to

`P(k) ∝ exp(beta * logit_k)`

with `logit0 = 0`. The likelihood therefore identifies only the products
`beta * logit_k`, not `beta` and the logits separately.
For any positive constant `c`, replacing `beta` with `c * beta`
and each logit with `logit_k / c` leaves the likelihood unchanged.

For plain choice-only fits, we recommend fixing `beta` (for example, at
`1.0`) rather than estimating it jointly with the logits. When `beta` is
free, its posterior can remain strongly influenced by its prior, and
sampling may show divergences and poor convergence.

This limitation does not apply in the same way to reinforcement-learning
(RL) presets: when the values entering the softmax are generated by the
learning process rather than treated as free logit parameters, `beta`
can be identified from the choices as those values change over time.

## Specialized model families

The table covers the `hssm.HSSM(model=...)` registry. Two specialized public
classes have separate configuration and discovery surfaces:

- [`hssm.aDDM`](../api/addm.md) models attentional evidence accumulation from
  trial and fixation data.
- [`hssm.RLSSM`](../api/rl.md) combines reinforcement-learning updates with an
  SSM observation model; its class-level and module-level `list_models`
  functions enumerate the RL registry.

Use [`hssm.list_models`](../api/model_registry.md) to discover the built-in HSSM
names programmatically. Custom HSSM configurations can be added with
[`hssm.register_model`](../api/model_registry.md).

For the conceptual difference between the likelihood kinds, see
[Likelihood kinds in HSSM](../explanations/likelihoods.md).
